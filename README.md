# 🚀 Mathematics for Machine Learning

### A Self-Study Journey Through Mathematical Foundations, Conceptual Understanding & Interactive Visualization

Welcome to my repository dedicated to learning and mastering the
mathematical foundations required for Machine Learning, Artificial
Intelligence, and future research.

Mathematics is the backbone of Machine Learning. My goal is not simply
to learn mathematical formulas or solve problems, but to develop a deep
understanding of the underlying concepts, their mathematical
foundations, and their practical interpretations.

This repository documents my ongoing learning journey, from Linear
Algebra to Calculus and other essential mathematical topics for Machine
Learning.

## 🧠 My Learning & Documentation Methodology

My approach focuses on active learning, first-principles understanding,
and visual exploration rather than simply collecting lecture notes.

### 1. Manual Note-Taking

While watching each lecture, I take handwritten notes, recording
mathematical steps, equations, important observations, and concepts.

This process helps me stay engaged with the material instead of
passively watching lectures.

### 2. First-Principles Understanding

I try to understand why a mathematical concept works rather than
memorizing its formula.

I revisit confusing concepts, examine their mathematical foundations,
work through examples, and explore alternative interpretations until I
develop a satisfactory understanding.

### 3. Confusion Clearing & Conceptual Exploration

An important part of this repository is documenting the questions and
difficulties I encounter during my learning process.

This includes:

-   Common conceptual misunderstandings.
-   Questions that arise while following the lectures.
-   Step-by-step explanations of difficult mathematical ideas.
-   Alternative interpretations and additional examples.
-   Geometric intuition behind abstract mathematical concepts.
-   Mathematical derivations that help clarify important results.

I believe documenting confusion is just as valuable as documenting what
I have already understood. A question that initially challenges me might
also help another learner facing the same difficulty.

### 4. AI-Assisted Documentation

After completing a lecture and preparing handwritten notes, I use AI
tools to assist with organizing, formatting, and structuring the
material into professional Jupyter Notebooks.

The workflow may involve providing lecture references, handwritten
notes, and additional instructions to help generate well-organized
mathematical documentation.

AI is used as a supporting tool for documentation and presentation. I
review the resulting content and work toward verifying the mathematical
explanations and calculations.

### 5. Interactive Mathematical Visualization

Mathematics becomes easier to understand when abstract ideas can be
explored visually.

Where appropriate, I develop interactive visualizations using Python and
related libraries.

**Tools and Technologies:** - Python - NumPy - Matplotlib - Plotly -
Jupyter Notebook

The visualizations may include interactive 2D and 3D graphs, geometric
interpretations, matrix transformations, and other mathematical
demonstrations.

## 💻 How to View and Run the Notebooks

You can explore the notebooks directly on GitHub or execute them using
Google Colab.

**Using Google Colab:**

1.  Navigate to the lecture folder you want to explore.
2.  Open the desired `.ipynb` file.
3.  Select or upload the notebook in [Google
    Colab](https://colab.research.google.com/).
4.  Run the cells to execute the examples and explore the
    visualizations.

Some interactive visualizations may require executing the notebook cells
and installing the relevant Python dependencies.
